# ARIA Hackathon Demo Script

## Opening

Environmental decisions combine observations, science, location, time, regulation, and human judgment. That information is usually fragmented. ARIA preserves it as a traceable chain from evidence to decision support.

## Prototype demonstration

1. Open **Balanced evidence**.
2. Run the assessment.
3. Point to the five separate domain findings and the absence of a hidden composite score.
4. Open the evidence ledger and show source, date, location, method, and quality.
5. Switch to **Missing water evidence** and show that ARIA reports insufficient evidence rather than inventing certainty.
6. Switch to **Regulatory restriction** and show that the system stops the scenario rather than allowing AI reasoning to bypass the constraint.
7. Show the human review gates and limitations.

## IBM Bob development story

Show only evidence the team actually recorded:

1. the repository and workflow before Bob
2. the task contract given to Bob
3. Bob's documented understanding and plan
4. actual Agent Mode, subagent, parallel task, or document understanding work
5. tests and review findings
6. measured before and after results

Do not attribute changes to Bob unless Bob actually produced or coordinated them.

## Closing

ARIA does not replace scientists, regulators, or environmental professionals. It makes evidence, uncertainty, and constraints visible enough to support better human decisions. IBM Bob helps the team build and validate that complex workflow with less coordination friction.
